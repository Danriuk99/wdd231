NP Membership Benefits

Designed for non-profit organizations in Monterrey with no membership fee:

  • Listing in the Chamber member directory
  • Access to community networking mixers
  • Invitations to public policy and civic workshops

Bronze Membership Benefits

Essential business visibility and networking for growing companies:

  • All NP Membership benefits included
  • Standard directory profile with business logo
  • 15% discount on Chamber networking events and seminars

Silver Membership Benefits

Expanded promotion and training opportunities for established businesses:

  • All Bronze Membership benefits included
  • Eligibility for Home page spotlight features
  • 25% discount on workshops, training sessions, and trade events

Gold Membership Benefits

Maximum visibility, priority access, and executive leadership perks:

  • All Silver Membership benefits included
  • Priority spotlight placement and featured promotions
  • VIP passes to annual Chamber summits and 40% event discount
